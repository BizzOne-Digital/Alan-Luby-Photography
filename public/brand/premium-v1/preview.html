Usage and small-size checks

Use the full signature at 360 px wide or larger so the photography descriptor stays readable. For compact navigation, profile images and small watermarks, use the monogram at 48 px or larger; 32 px is suitable for high-contrast monochrome use. Preserve clear space around every asset. Use the pure black or white masters for single-colour reproduction. Warm ivory is an application colour; all master logo backgrounds are transparent.

Black horizontal · White horizontal · Black monogram · White monogram · Image generation prompts
